Our Story

Austin-Born,
Rooted in Craft

Founded in 2011 by Marcus and Elena Reyes, Verdant Landscapes grew from a two-person operation into one of Austin's most trusted outdoor design firms. We believe a great yard is one you actually use.

Every project begins with a complimentary consultation where we listen first. We understand Texas soil, Hill Country drainage, and the unique palette of native plants that thrive here — so our designs don't just look stunning on day one, they improve with every season.

Our crews are background-checked, trained in-house, and take genuine pride in leaving your property cleaner than they found it.
